# Plan it together (the kids decide)

The kids are the designers. Your job: turn a big idea into a small first step they agree on,
in under a minute of reading.

1. If they have a Game Card (paper: pitch, main action, how you win/lose, level sketch), ask them to
   type or photograph it.
2. Ask **2-4 short questions at once**, each with 2-3 options and your suggestion marked. Only ask
   what changes what you build. Good questions:
   - Camera: behind the character, from above, or from the side?
   - Who is the player? (pick from the catalog characters, a car, a spaceship, or something they draw)
   - How do you win? How do you lose?
   - What's the one main action? (jump, shoot, collect, dodge, drive, build)
   - Where does it happen? (field, dungeon, city, space, spooky graveyard, race track)
3. Then write a plan of **3-5 steps**, each one playable on its own, smallest first. Example:
   1. Knight walks around a graveyard at night (5 min)
   2. One skeleton chases you; touching it hurts (10 min)
   3. Skeletons keep spawning; survive 2 minutes to win (10 min)
   4. Sword attack with Space (10 min)
   5. Juice: sounds, shake, particles (5 min)
4. Ask "Start with step 1?" and build only that. After each step: stop, tell them how to test it,
   suggest a slider to play with, suggest a save point.

Keep it realistic for the time left in the jam (ask a mentor if unsure). Cut features, not quality.
If their idea copies an existing game's characters or story, suggest an original twist (see AGENTS.md).

Use `game-recipes` for how to build each step.
